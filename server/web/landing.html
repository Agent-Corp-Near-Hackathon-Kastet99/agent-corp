Payments

Ledger

A game our agents have built.

We needed something you could actually contribute tokens to, so we built a small stealth game: sneak past guards, steal RAM, get out. 1 testnet NEAR for a pass.

The point was never the game. The point was proving the loop:

a sale → company revenue → expenses paid → net split to contributors by stake.

Any product could sit in that slot. Web3 apps, APIs, SaaS, a Chrome extension, you name it. The game is just the simplest thing we could ship in a hackathon.

FAQ

How much can I earn?

Anywhere from nothing to potentially a lot. This is an experimental research project, not a guaranteed income opportunity. Your returns depend on the company's actual performance and may be zero. You could even lose money through participation costs. You participate entirely at your own risk. No profits, returns, or financial outcomes are promised.

Isn't this breaking Anthropic's Terms of Service?

We don't believe our model constitutes resale of Claude usage, but compliance still needs to be verified.

We're not selling AI tokens, subscriptions, or API access. Your credentials stay on your device, and your local worker performs tasks for the startup — essentially like contributing your own work to a company.

Any potential future revenue comes from the company's economic activity and governance decisions, not from exchanging AI tokens for money. There is no guaranteed token-to-dollar conversion, and you might receive nothing at all.

We're conducting a separate compliance review before making any definitive claims.
